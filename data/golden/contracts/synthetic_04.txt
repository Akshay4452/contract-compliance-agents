DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM
(Synthetic golden contract — mostly adequate controls.)

This DPA is between Customer and CleanProcess GmbH ("Processor").

1. DEFINITIONS.
"Customer Data" means personal data Customer provides for processing under this DPA.

2. SUBPROCESSORS.
Processor shall not engage any subprocessor without Customer's prior written approval. Processor will provide thirty (30) days' advance written notice of any proposed new subprocessor and a mechanism for Customer to object.

3. DATA RETENTION.
Processor retains Customer Data only for the term of the Services and shall delete or return it when no longer needed for the contracted purpose, unless law requires longer retention.

4. BREACH NOTIFICATION.
Processor shall notify Customer of a personal data breach without undue delay and within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware, including known facts and mitigation steps.

5. LIABILITY.
Processor's aggregate liability under this DPA shall not exceed the fees paid by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

6. RETURN OF DATA.
Within thirty (30) days after termination, Processor shall return Customer Data in a commonly used format or securely delete it and certify deletion in writing upon request.
