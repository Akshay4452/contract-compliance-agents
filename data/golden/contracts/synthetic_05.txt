VENDOR CLOUD AGREEMENT
(Synthetic golden contract — retention and liability gaps.)

Agreement between Customer and KeepForever Systems, Inc. ("Vendor").

1. SERVICES.
Vendor provides hosted workflow software as described in the Order Form.

2. FEES.
Fees are due within forty-five (45) days of invoice and are non-refundable.

3. SUBPROCESSORS.
Vendor shall obtain Customer's prior written consent before appointing a subprocessor and will maintain an up-to-date subprocessor list.

4. DATA RETENTION.
Vendor may store Customer personal data indefinitely for product improvement and marketing analytics, with no obligation to delete when the data is no longer needed for the Services.

5. SECURITY INCIDENTS.
Vendor shall notify Customer of a confirmed personal data breach within forty-eight (48) hours and provide reasonable cooperation.

6. LIABILITY.
The parties' remedies are those available at law. This Agreement does not include any fees-based or dollar maximum liability cap.

7. EXIT AND DATA.
Upon termination, Vendor shall return or securely delete Customer personal data within thirty (30) days of Customer's written request, except copies retained solely as required by law.
