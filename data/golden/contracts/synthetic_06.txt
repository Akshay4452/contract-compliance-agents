SOFTWARE SERVICES AGREEMENT
(Synthetic golden contract — open sharing and weak breach notice.)

This Agreement is between Customer and RelayNet Corp. ("Provider").

1. PREAMBLE.
Provider will host and operate the messaging platform for Customer.

2. THIRD PARTY PROCESSORS.
Provider may freely share Customer personal data with any vendor, affiliate, or partner Provider chooses, without prior notice to or approval by Customer.

3. RETENTION POLICY.
Provider deletes Customer personal data within ninety (90) days after the Services end, unless a longer period is required by law.

4. INCIDENT HANDLING.
If Provider learns of a personal data breach, Provider may inform Customer when convenient. There is no required notification deadline.

5. DAMAGES CAP.
Provider's total liability arising out of this Agreement shall not exceed twelve (12) months of fees paid by Customer immediately before the claim.

6. DATA ON TERMINATION.
On termination, Provider will return or delete Customer personal data within thirty (30) days after written request and certify deletion if asked.
