HOSTED ANALYTICS DPA
(Synthetic golden contract — exit/return gap only.)

Between Customer and MetricBox Ltd. ("Processor").

1. SCOPE.
Processor processes Customer personal data solely to deliver the analytics Service.

2. SUBPROCESSOR CONTROLS.
Processor will not engage subprocessors without prior written approval and will give thirty (30) days' notice before any change to the approved list.

3. STORAGE LIMITATION.
Processor keeps Customer personal data only while the Order Form is active and deletes it from production systems within thirty (30) days after Customer requests deletion.

4. BREACH TIMING.
Processor notifies Customer of personal data breaches without undue delay and within seventy-two (72) hours of awareness, with available details.

5. LIABILITY LIMIT.
Aggregate liability is capped at fees paid in the prior twelve (12) months, excluding willful misconduct.

6. END OF ENGAGEMENT.
When this DPA ends, Processor may retain Customer personal data in backups indefinitely and has no duty to return or delete that data at Customer's request.
