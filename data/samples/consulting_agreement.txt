PROFESSIONAL SERVICES CONSULTING AGREEMENT

This Consulting Agreement (the "Agreement") is dated 8 February 2026 between Lark & Quill Advisory Ltd ("Consultant") and Bright Harbor Payments Ltd ("Client"). Consultant will provide data-protection programme review services described in a statement of work.

1. SERVICES AND DELIVERABLES.

Consultant shall perform the services with reasonable skill and care. Deliverables are for Client's internal use. Consultant may use anonymized know-how in future work.

2. FEES.

Client shall pay the daily rates in the statement of work plus pre-approved expenses. Invoices are due in fourteen (14) days.

3. CLIENT MATERIALS.

Client remains owner of all Client materials. Consultant shall use Client materials only to perform the services and shall not disclose them to third parties.

4. PERSONAL DATA.

If Consultant processes Personal Data for Client, Consultant does so as a processor and only on Client's instructions. Consultant shall not appoint a subprocessor without Client's prior written consent. Upon request, Consultant shall return or delete Personal Data within fourteen (14) days after the engagement ends.

5. TERM AND EXIT.

Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice, or immediately for material breach not cured within ten (10) days. On exit, Consultant shall deliver work in progress, return Client materials, and delete Client Personal Data as set out in Section 4.

6. INDEMNIFICATION.

Consultant shall indemnify Client against third-party claims arising from Consultant's negligence or willful misconduct in performing the services. Client shall indemnify Consultant against claims arising from Client materials or Client instructions.

7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY.

Consultant's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid by Client in the six (6) months before the claim, except for fraud, death or personal injury caused by negligence, or Consultant's indemnification obligations. Neither party is liable for lost profits or indirect damages.

8. GOVERNING LAW.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Scotland.
