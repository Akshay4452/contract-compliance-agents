VENDOR SECURITY ADDENDUM

This Vendor Security Addendum (the "Addendum") supplements the services agreement between Northline Analytics Ltd ("Customer") and Packetwell Hosting Ltd ("Vendor") dated 20 January 2026. If there is a conflict on security or data handling, this Addendum controls.

1. SECURITY PROGRAM.

Vendor shall maintain a written information security program aligned with SOC 2 Type II trust services criteria for security and availability. Upon request, Vendor shall provide Customer with its latest SOC 2 Type II report under NDA.

2. ACCESS CONTROL.

Vendor shall enforce unique user credentials, multi-factor authentication for remote access to systems that process Customer data, and least-privilege access reviews at least quarterly.

3. SUBPROCESSORS AND THIRD-PARTY SHARING.

Vendor shall not share Customer data with a third party except subprocessors approved under the DPA. Vendor shall flow down equivalent security obligations and remain responsible for subprocessors.

4. INCIDENT RESPONSE.

Vendor shall notify Customer of a security incident affecting Customer data within seventy-two (72) hours of confirmation, and shall reasonably cooperate in investigation and remediation.

5. DATA RETURN ON EXIT.

When the services end, Vendor shall, within thirty (30) days of Customer's written request, return Customer data in a standard format and then securely delete remaining copies, except as required by law.

6. AUDIT RIGHTS.

Customer may, not more than once per year, review Vendor's relevant policies and, where reasonably necessary, conduct an on-site or remote audit with fifteen (15) days' notice.

7. LIABILITY.

Nothing in this Addendum increases the liability cap in the main services agreement, except that Vendor's liability for a proven unauthorized disclosure of Customer data caused by Vendor's failure to meet Section 1 or Section 2 shall not be less than twelve (12) months of fees.
