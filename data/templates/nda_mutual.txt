MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of 1 March 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between Northline Analytics Ltd ("Northline") and Harbor Peak Systems Inc. ("Harbor Peak"). Each party may disclose Confidential Information to the other in connection with evaluating a potential software integration (the "Purpose").

1. DEFINITIONS.

"Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by a party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other (the "Receiving Party"), whether in writing, orally, or by inspection of materials, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure. Confidential Information includes product roadmaps, customer lists, pricing, security architecture, and personal data shared for the Purpose.

2. OBLIGATIONS.

The Receiving Party shall (a) use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose, (b) protect it using at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information of like importance, and in no event less than reasonable care, and (c) not disclose it to third parties except to employees and contractors who have a need to know and are bound by written confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Agreement.

3. EXCLUSIONS.

The obligations in Section 2 do not apply to information that the Receiving Party can document (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the Receiving Party, (b) was rightfully in its possession without confidentiality duty, (c) is independently developed without use of Confidential Information, or (d) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction.

4. TERM AND RETURN.

This Agreement continues for two (2) years from the Effective Date. Upon written request, or automatically upon expiry, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or securely destroy Confidential Information, except that one copy may be retained in backup systems if kept confidential and used only for legal compliance. Confidentiality obligations survive for three (3) years after termination, and indefinitely for trade secrets.

5. NO LICENSE.

No license to any intellectual property is granted except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose. Each party retains all rights in its own information.

6. GOVERNING LAW.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of London.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.
