SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made as of 15 January 2026 between Cloudspan Technologies Ltd ("Vendor") and Riverbend Retail Group plc ("Customer"). Vendor will provide the hosted analytics platform described in an Order Form (the "Service") and Customer will pay the fees set out in that Order Form.

1. SERVICES.

Vendor shall make the Service available to Customer during the Subscription Term in accordance with this Agreement and the applicable Order Form. Vendor may use subprocessors to host and operate the Service, provided Vendor remains responsible for their performance. A current list of subprocessors is available at Vendor's trust site; Vendor shall give Customer thirty (30) days' prior written notice before appointing a new subprocessor that processes Customer Personal Data.

2. CUSTOMER DATA.

Customer retains all right, title, and interest in data submitted to the Service ("Customer Data"). Vendor shall process Customer Personal Data only as a processor on documented instructions from Customer, as further described in the Data Processing Addendum. Vendor shall not sell Customer Data.

3. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION.

During the Subscription Term, Customer Data is retained for as long as Customer keeps it in the Service. Within thirty (30) days after termination or expiry, Vendor shall delete Customer Data from production systems or return it in a commonly used machine-readable format, at Customer's written election, except for copies in encrypted backups which are overwritten on Vendor's standard backup cycle not to exceed ninety (90) days. Vendor shall not retain Customer Personal Data longer than necessary for the purposes of this Agreement.

4. FEES AND PAYMENT.

Customer shall pay the fees in each Order Form within thirty (30) days of invoice. Late amounts accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum allowed by law, whichever is less. Fees are exclusive of taxes.

5. TERM AND TERMINATION.

This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for the Subscription Term. Either party may terminate for material breach if the breach remains uncured thirty (30) days after written notice. Upon termination, Customer shall stop using the Service. Vendor shall, at Customer's request, return or delete Customer Data as described in Section 3 and certify deletion in writing.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY.

Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information in confidence and use it only to perform this Agreement. These duties survive for three (3) years after termination.

7. WARRANTIES.

Vendor warrants that the Service will perform materially in accordance with the documentation. Customer's exclusive remedy for a breach of this warranty is, at Vendor's option, re-performance or a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees for the non-conforming period.

8. DISCLAIMER.

EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED, THE SERVICE IS PROVIDED "AS IS." VENDOR DISCLAIMS ALL IMPLIED WARRANTIES, INCLUDING MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE, AND NON-INFRINGEMENT.

9. INDEMNIFICATION.

Vendor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Customer against third-party claims that the Service, as provided by Vendor, infringes a patent, copyright, or trademark, and against claims arising from Vendor's unauthorized processing of Customer Personal Data. Customer shall indemnify Vendor against claims arising from Customer Data or Customer's misuse of the Service. The indemnifying party may control the defense, and the indemnified party shall reasonably cooperate. This is who pays legal costs if a third party sues because of the other party's product or data handling.

10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY.

10.1 Liability cap. Except for (a) a party's indemnification obligations under Section 9, (b) a party's breach of confidentiality, and (c) Customer's payment obligations, each party's total aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable by Customer to Vendor in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the claim. That cap is the maximum payout if things go wrong.

10.2 Excluded damages. Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages, or for lost profits, lost revenue, or lost data, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

11. BREACH NOTIFICATION.

Vendor shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data, and shall provide information reasonably required for Customer to meet its own notification duties.

12. GENERAL.

This Agreement is the entire agreement on its subject matter. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties. Notices may be sent by email to the addresses on the Order Form. Governing law: England and Wales.
